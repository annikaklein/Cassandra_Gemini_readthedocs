Funding
-------

The Junior Research Group (JRG) "Future Urban Coastlines" is funded by the German Research
Foundation (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft, DFG) as part of the Excellence Strategy of the
Federal and State Governments – UP 8/1. The authors further acknowledge funding from the Federal
Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR; project "Marine Extremes Transforming
coAsts - pathways for coastal adaptation across scales" or METAscales, FKZ 03F0955A) and the
Northern German states within the scope of the German Marine Research Alliance (DAM) mission
mareXtreme "Pathways to improved risk management in the area of marine extreme events and
natural hazards".

The authors acknowledge open-access funding under Project DEAL, a German initiative that
supports open-access academic publishing.
